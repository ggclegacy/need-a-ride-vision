# Need A Ride — Digital Vision Experience

A private, cinematic product-vision experience for Need A Ride LLC.

The current build contains the Stage 1 arrival foundation, the Stage 3
customer product concept, the Stage 4 Owner Command Center concept, and the
Stage 5 Growth Opportunities concept, the Stage 6 Phased Roadmap concept, and
the Stage 7 Operations Platform reveal:

- Global design and motion foundations
- Cinematic environmental background
- Opening arrival scene
- Entry transition
- Lightweight progress and back navigation
- Responsive and accessibility foundations
- Guided pickup, destination, timing, and trip-detail flow
- Deterministic local concept estimate
- Booking review and Square-inspired demo payment
- Ride confirmation, status, and communication previews
- Executive pullback into a local operations demonstration
- Owner overview with schedule, attention queue, and sample metrics
- Interactive ride board with assignment and status controls
- Driver readiness and customer relationship previews
- Connected payment visibility and reporting concepts
- Dedicated driver mobile-app concept with assignment, schedule, status, and
  performance previews
- Fleet intelligence with vehicle readiness, maintenance, inspection,
  registration, insurance, and service-history concepts
- Dispatch command timeline with assignments, ride states, and communication
  previews
- Customer CRM with relationship profiles, ride history, service notes, and
  business-account concepts
- Analytics dashboard with clearly fictional revenue, utilization, repeat
  customer, and destination trend data
- Future Integrations portfolio covering live GPS, flight tracking, AI,
  corporate accounts, customer mobile apps, and advanced automation
- Guided opportunity portfolio across demand, partnerships, and accounts
- Interactive discovery shortlist with owner-first evaluation questions
- Focused experiment concepts without fabricated growth projections
- Interactive Foundation → Operations → Expansion build sequence
- Milestone dependencies, decision gates, and owner discovery agenda
- Growth-shortlist continuity into the controlled Expansion pilot
- Phase 2 owner-intake handoff for the next build stage

No live quote, payment, map, booking, dispatch, driver, messaging, GPS, fleet,
maintenance, customer-data, analytics, partnership, account, AI, or backend
services are implemented.
